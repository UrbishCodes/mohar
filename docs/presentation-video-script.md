# Mohar — Presentation Video Script (DRAFT)

**Target:** 2–3 minutes (the WHY — this is the first thing judges watch)
**Format:** Urbish on camera; product screenshare where marked

---

## 1. Hook (0:00–0:25)

"Before the rupee, Nepal's money was the silver mohar. I'm bringing the mohar
back — as the seal on every freelance deal.

I'm Urbish, a developer from Kathmandu, and I built Mohar: escrow for
freelancers, on Solana."

## 2. Problem (0:25–1:05)

"Millions of freelancers do their best work for clients they've never met —
and then the invoice goes unpaid. Late payments. Ghosted clients. Zero
leverage. Platforms take up to 20% and still leave you exposed; direct-client
work, where the margins are best, has no protection at all.

In Nepal, this is the daily reality for thousands of freelancers earning
global money from their bedrooms. The trust gap is literally a tax on their
income."

## 3. Solution (1:05–1:50)

*[Screenshare: the Mohar app]*

"Mohar closes the gap with a smart-contract escrow. The client locks USDC
when work starts. The freelancer delivers and marks it complete. The client
approves — funds release instantly.

If the client ghosts, the freelancer claims after the deadline. No more
'I'll pay next week.' If there's a real disagreement, a neutral arbiter
resolves it on-chain. Every step is transparent, every fund movement is
verifiable."

## 4. Why Solana / why now (1:50–2:15)

"A $50 gig can't absorb high gas fees. Solana's sub-cent fees and instant
finality make micro-escrows viable for the first time. And the users are
already here — Nepal ranks 4th globally in this hackathon's registrations.
The builders are here. The freelancers are here. The rails weren't — until now."

## 5. Why me + vision (2:15–2:50)

"I'm the user. I'm a Nepali developer earning globally, and I built the
rails I wish existed. Solo-built, AI-assisted — the fastest team in 2026 is
you and your agent.

The vision: Mohar becomes the trust layer for freelance work — milestone
releases, on-chain reputation, and eventually direct settlement for
freelancers everywhere. Sealed. Settled. Paid."

*[End card: Mohar logo — a stamped seal — + repo link]*
