The problem: Nepali freelancers work with clients across the world, and they constantly get burned. Late payments. Ghosted invoices. Zero leverage when a client disappears.

Mohar fixes it simply: when work starts, the client locks USDC in a smart-contract escrow. The freelancer delivers. The client approves — and the money releases instantly. If the client ghosts, the freelancer can claim after the deadline. If there's a dispute, a neutral arbiter resolves it on-chain.

My users are the thousands of Nepali freelancers on Upwork and Fiverr losing money to broken trust.

Why am I the right person to build this? Because I *am* the user. I'm a Nepali developer earning globally — and I'm building the rails I wish already existed."
