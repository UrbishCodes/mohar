# Mohar — Go-to-Market Strategy & Distribution Plan

## The problem (demand)

Nepali freelancers working with global clients face a trust gap with no good
solution: late payments, ghosted invoices, and zero leverage when a client
disappears. Upwork/Fiverr take 10–20% cuts and still leave payment disputes
slow and painful. Direct-client work — where margins are best — has no
protection at all.

**Demand validation:**
- The founder is the user: a Nepali developer earning globally who has lived
  this problem.
- Adjacent products exist and get traction (Pulsepay — freelancer payments on
  Solana; Solsync — onchain payroll), proving willingness to move freelance
  money on-chain. None are built for the Nepali freelancer's reality:
  USDC earnings with an NPR off-ramp, bilingual UX, local community trust.
- Nepal ranks 4th globally in Colosseum Crypto World's Fair registrations
  (514 builders) — the builder density is real, and freelancing is one of the
  top income paths for that cohort.

## Target users (in order)

1. **Nepali freelancers** on Upwork/Fiverr and direct clients (10–50k
   addressable, high pain, reachable through local communities).
2. **South Asian freelancers** (India, Bangladesh, Pakistan) — same pain,
   same off-ramp patterns, English-first UX already works.
3. **Global crypto-native freelancers** — DAOs, grant recipients, bounty
   hunters who already hold USDC.

## Distribution plan

- **Superteam Nepal community** — Buildstation coworking, Demo Day in
  Kathmandu, Telegram/Discord channels. This is where the first 100 users
  come from: builders who freelance between gigs.
- **Freelancer communities** — Nepali freelancer Facebook groups, r/Nepal,
  Upwork/Fiverr community forums. Content-led: "stop getting ghosted"
  guides with Mohar walkthroughs.
- **The anti-brain-drain video itself** — the bounty submission doubles as
  launch content (X, LinkedIn, TikTok), telling the exact story of the user.
- **Integration partnerships** — bounty platforms (Superteam Earn itself),
  grant programs, and DAOs that pay contributors in USDC are natural
  escrow customers.
- **Arbiter network** — reputable community members stake reputation as
  arbiters, bringing their own followings.

## Business model

- **1% escrow fee** on released funds (below Upwork's 10%, above zero —
  the trust premium). Waived/reduced for high-volume users.
- **Arbiter fees** — small cut of dispute resolution fees as the network grows.
- Target: 1,000 monthly active escrows × $500 average × 1% = **$5,000 MRR**
  at initial scale; the model compounds with volume, not headcount.

## Roadmap

- **Now (hackathon MVP):** create / fund / deliver / release / refund /
  dispute / deadline-claim on Solana devnet, React frontend.
- **Next:** milestone-based partial releases, on-chain reputation scores for
  freelancers and clients, email/wallet notifications.
- **Later:** mobile app, multi-token support, fiat off-ramp partnerships for
  direct NPR settlement, arbiter staking.

## Why Solana

Sub-cent fees and instant finality make micro-escrows viable — a $50 gig
can't absorb Ethereum gas. The Solana freelancer-tooling ecosystem (wallets,
USDC rails, Superteam communities) is where the users already are.
